🚊 What We Do

TransitEase helps commuters find optimal routes, estimate accurate travel times, distances, and fares, and navigate public transportation with confidence through clear, real-time travel information across all NCR cities.

Our platform provides step-by-step directions for jeepneys, buses, MRT/LRT trains, and walking routes—combining multiple transport modes for the most efficient journey.

💡 Commuter Tips

Make your commute safer and more efficient with these expert-recommended tips for navigating Metro Manila's public transportation system.

  • Avoid rush hours (7-9 AM, 5-8 PM) when traffic is heaviest
  • Plan routes ahead using TransitEase's smart route finder
  • Prepare exact fare or load your BEEP card before boarding
  • Use Fare Calculator to estimate costs before traveling
  • Stay alert in crowded areas and keep belongings secure
  • Use MRT/LRT during rush hour for faster travel
  • Check traffic status before departing to avoid congestion
  • Keep emergency contacts readily accessible on your phone
  • Share your location with someone when traveling at night
